About

Lights is the show-control app for this basement rig. Pick a scene and it drives the DMX fixtures and WLED strips. One operator, one machine, one LAN.

Modes

Performance is live control: scene tiles, spacebar beats, deactivate, and blackout.

Builder is where you author fixture looks, pair them into a dmx_preset, build cue lists, and save scenes.

The rig

Chauvet GigBAR 2 23CH, universe 1 ch 1–23
Keobin light bar 18CH, ch 24–41
WLED / LEDfx strips scene names
ILDA laser storage only, not live

How to run a show

  1. Build device presets for each fixture.
  2. Pair GigBAR + Keobin into a look (dmx_preset).
  3. Order looks into cue lists. Same for WLED.
  4. Pair a DMX list with a WLED list to make a scene.
  5. Open Performance, activate a scene. Spacebar taps a beat until audio lands.

Notes

  • Blackout is the panic button when the room needs to go dark.
  • The GigBAR has a laser — don’t stare into it or point it at faces.
  • Output is unicast on universe 1. If the LAN dies, the looks stop.
  • Beats are still a tap until live audio is wired.
